# PocketSmart AI – User Guide

## Step 1 – Open the Application

Open the PocketSmart AI application in a web browser.

## Step 2 – Add Income

1. Go to the Add Income section.
2. Enter the income amount.
3. Select the income source.
4. Click Add Income.

The income will be added to the dashboard.

## Step 3 – Add Expense

1. Go to the Add Expense section.
2. Enter the expense amount.
3. Select the expense category.
4. Enter a description.
5. Click Add Expense.

The expense will appear in Recent Transactions.

## Step 4 – Set Monthly Budget

1. Open Set Monthly Budget.
2. Enter the budget amount.
3. Click Set Budget.

The monthly budget will appear on the dashboard.

## Step 5 – View Balance

The dashboard automatically displays:

- Total Income
- Total Expenses
- Remaining Balance
- Monthly Budget

## Step 6 – View Recommendation

After entering expenses and setting a budget, the recommendation section provides a spending recommendation based on the budget usage.

## Step 7 – Check Transactions

The Recent Transactions section displays income and expense records.

## Conclusion

PocketSmart AI can be used to track personal income and expenses and monitor monthly spending.
